Privacy Policy

Summary

DriveOS Launcher does not create user accounts, show advertising, use AdMob, or operate developer analytics or remote crash-reporting services. Most settings, diagnostics, backups, recent destinations, and route data stay on your device.

Permissions and optional access

  • Approximate and precise location: used for GPS navigation and location-based weather after you grant Android permission.
  • Internet and network state: used for map tiles, routing, destination search, weather, and connectivity status.
  • Foreground location service: used while active route tracking is running and shown through an Android foreground-service notification.
  • Notification listener: optional special access used to show media information and compatible external navigation notifications. DriveOS does not send notification contents to a developer-operated server.

DriveOS does not request Android background-location permission.

Data sent to third parties

Only when you use the applicable online feature, the app may send current, start, destination, and route coordinates to the configured OSRM-compatible routing service; destination-search text to a Nominatim-compatible geocoding service; location coordinates to Open-Meteo for weather; and map-tile coordinates plus standard request metadata to the selected map-tile service.

When the optional Google Maps basemap is enabled, Google may receive map requests, IP address, device and request metadata, a Maps-specific pseudonymous identifier, SDK crash information, and map interaction events. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

DriveOS may store app preferences, assigned and hidden apps, plugin settings, local diagnostic logs, platform-discovery snapshots, recent destinations, saved home and work points, a short-lived route cache, and user-imported offline map files. A backup file is created only when you explicitly export one through Android's document picker.

Retention and your choices

Local data remains until it expires under an app cache limit, is replaced, is cleared in Android settings, or the app is uninstalled. You can decline optional permissions and avoid online location features. Third-party service providers control their own data retention.

Children and driving safety

DriveOS is intended for adults configuring compatible vehicle head units. Configure the launcher, permissions, and destinations while parked and avoid interacting with a device while driving.
