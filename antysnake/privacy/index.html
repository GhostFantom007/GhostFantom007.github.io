Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AntiSnake handles information when you use the game.

1. Information processed

AntiSnake does not require an account. The game may store game preferences and progress locally on your device. When advertisements are shown, advertising partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address, app interaction information and approximate location derived from IP address, subject to your device settings and applicable law.

2. Advertising

AntiSnake uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to provide advertising. Google and its partners may use information to serve, measure and improve advertisements. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Legal bases and choices

Where required, the game requests consent before serving personalised ads. You can manage advertising preferences in your device settings, and you may reset your device advertising identifier at any time. Some non-personalised ads may still be shown.

4. Data retention and security

Local game data remains on your device until you remove the game or clear its storage. Third-party providers retain information according to their own policies. We use reasonable measures to protect the game, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Children

AntiSnake is not directed to children below the minimum age required to consent to data processing in their country. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, please contact that provider directly.

6. Your rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information. Requests related to advertising data should be sent to the relevant advertising provider. For questions about AntiSnake, use the developer contact details displayed on Google Play.

7. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the game or applicable law. The revised policy will be published here with a new effective date.
