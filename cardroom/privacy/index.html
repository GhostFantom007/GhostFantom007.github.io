Kartarnya — Card Games

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Kartarnya is an offline card-game app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

1. Information stored on the device

Kartarnya does not require an account. Game settings, preferences, statistics, and saved sessions are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this local game data.

2. Advertising

Kartarnya uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for optional rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch an ad to receive an in-game bonus, such as a hint or undo. The app does not show automatic ads during a match.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, technical device information, and ad interaction information to serve, measure, and protect advertising, subject to your device settings and applicable law. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Consent and choices

Where required, the app requests consent before ads are requested. You can manage available privacy choices in the app settings or through your device's advertising settings. If an ad is unavailable or not completed, the bonus is not granted.

4. Permissions

The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, location, or personal files. Internet access is used to load optional ads.

5. Children

Kartarnya is not directed specifically to children. AdMob may process advertising and technical data according to applicable consent settings and Google's policies.

6. Data deletion

To delete local settings, statistics, and saved sessions, uninstall Kartarnya or clear its app storage in Android settings. Advertising providers manage data according to their own policies and controls.

7. Updates and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. For questions, use the developer contact details shown on Google Play.
